History of Delhi

Delhi is one of the oldest and most important cities in India. It has been the capital of several historical kingdoms and empires.

Famous Places

Delhi is famous for the Red Fort, India Gate, Qutub Minar, Lotus Temple and many other historical monuments.

Famous Food

Delhi offers delicious foods such as chole bhature, parathas, kebabs and street food.
